WAR OF KINGDOMS

Choose Your War

Command fictional kingdoms in a fast territory-control battle. Claim neutral provinces, seize valuable resources, recruit armies, and outmaneuver your rivals.

Conquest ends after the chosen number of rounds. Most territory wins.

Each kingdom begins with a capital, a neighboring province, 6 armies and 5 gold. Neutral land can be claimed for 2 gold. The Crownheart is a prestigious central objective. Six special provinces generate gold each round. Every kingdom starts with exactly two territories, including in Quick War. Every turn gives 2 actions. Territory control determines victory.

🎮 Your turn: 2 actions

⚑ ClaimSpend 2 gold to take an adjacent neutral province. Move 1 army into it; leave at least 1 behind.
✚ RecruitSpend 1 gold per army, up to 2 armies per action; partial recruitment fills the remaining space.
⚔ AttackAttack an adjacent enemy province. Commit at least 2 armies, leaving at least 1 at home.
🛡 FortifyMove armies between adjacent friendly territories; leave at least 1 behind.
◈ RecoverGain 2 gold, up to the treasury limit of 15.
🤝 DiplomacyForm an alliance, transfer gold, trade across a shared border, sell a province, or break an alliance. Each action costs one turn action.

Choose an action, then tap the relevant province(s). Press End Turn when you're finished.

🎲 Battles & allies

Attack power = committed armies + allied attackers + one six-sided die.

Defense power = stationed armies + allied defenders + one six-sided die + capital/fortress bonuses.

Ties go to the defender. The attacker loses 1 committed army on a win. On a loss, the attacker loses up to 2 committed armies and the defender may lose 1.

Allied kingdoms can approve reinforcements from adjacent provinces on either side of a battle. Supporting armies remain in their own provinces but can take casualties. You cannot attack a current ally without first breaking the alliance.

🏆 Winning & economy

Conquest: Play 3, 5, or 7 rounds. Most territories wins; armies and then gold break ties. Total elimination can end the game early.

Last Kingdom Standing: No round limit. Eliminate every rival or, beginning in Round 3, secure unanimous peace among surviving kingdoms for a shared victory.

Turn income: 1 guaranteed gold + 2 per Gold Market + 3 per Gold Mine + 1 for Crownheart + 1 per active border trade pact. Gold is capped at 15.

Diplomacy: Trade pacts require a shared border and mutual agreement, but not an alliance. You may also sell non-capital border territories to another kingdom for an agreed price; the buyer must consent.

Fair starts: The game looks for openings with room to expand. Undo reverses the last action; Teacher offers corrections.

🤝 Council of Kingdoms

Treaties are public. Each kingdom still wins independently.

🛒 Trade & Territory

Trade partners must share a land border. A trade pact lasts through the next round and earns each partner +1 gold at the beginning of their turns. It does not create a military alliance.

Sell one of your non-capital provinces to another kingdom. The buyer must border it and approve the deal. Its former garrison disbands; the buyer receives 1 defending army. The sale uses one action.

🛡 Joint Attacks

No reservations needed. When an ally attacks a province bordering your armies, they can request your support at the battle itself. You decide whether to help.
